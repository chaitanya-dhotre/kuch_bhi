Page 10, round 2: S1 that reads as a capybara, plus five ideas

You picked S1 Sticker and said it looked like a brown seal. Round 2 redraws it from what makes the reference stickers read as capybaras. Every picture is a headless Rive CLI render of docs/design/mascot/styles/s1v2/scene.rml (4 artboards, no gradients, no blur). D2–D8 stay at their defaults. The ideas in band 3 are what I'd change, some of which break earlier rules.

1

Four views

What changed from the seal

  • Long, blunt, box-ended snout sticking out in front of the head, the strongest capybara cue.
  • Dark muzzle patch at the snout tip, not a round patch mid-face (that was the seal/bear read).
  • Small eye high and far back on the head, with the nostril as a slit at the top of the tip.
  • Flat head top, no neck: the head runs straight into a bean body.
  • Small round ears at the back of the head, two of them in profile.
  • Toe ticks on stubby feet, a thigh line, two fur ticks on the back.
  • Front view: wide flat head, eyes far apart at the sides, tall muzzle block, two nostril slits, split-lip mouth.
  • The profile faces left as drawn; Rive flips it for free, so in a banner it faces the text.
