S7

The collage (C17; decision 16)

Theme background, not black: it is a screen you scroll. Tap a tile → fullscreen at that item. Long-press a tile → Reply / Select / Delete for me / Delete for everyone for that item. Select (decision 27) turns the bar into a selection bar; taps toggle items; the bin deletes them together (for everyone only when all are yours). A late item appears; one left → the screen pops back to the chat.
S8

Collage layout rules (decision 17)

albumLayout(aspects, width), pure and deterministic, walked in pick order:

  1. Landscape (aspect ≥ 1.2): its own full-width row, height = width ÷ aspect, cropped to at most 2:1.
  2. Portrait or square: paired with the next item if that one is also portrait or square; widths in proportion to their aspects, one shared height.
  3. A lone portrait (next is landscape, or none): full width, cropped to at most 4:5.
  4. Unknown size (legacy, no metadata): aspect 1.0.

Gap 2dp. Every tile BoxFit.cover. Videos keep ▶ and their length chip. Mixed photo and video is just more tiles: the rule only reads the aspect.

Example: L, P, P, L, P, S
