Backlog 12.1. Photos, videos, files and text shared from other apps or a file manager land in a chosen chat and go through the existing send paths (media-send, albums). Sections S1–S10 (page-local). Picker states are drawn in light, dark and AMOLED side by side. Audit at 5580d1e, 2026-11-02. Checklist and decisions: share-in.md. Nothing here is built yet.

S1

Entry flows (B1–B7)

Cold startGallery → Share → kuch_bhi→app starts, takeShare once→splash→home + key OK→Send to
Warm startapp running (any screen, a draft open)→share arrives via onNewIntent (singleTask)→Send to on top→Back = the screen you were on, untouched
Signed outshare held in memory (nothing copied)→login + toast Sign in to share.→sign in (restore if asked)→Send to
Key restoreneedsRestore / needsSetup→restore or setup screen, no toast→home + key OK→Send to
Recentsapp killed after a share, reopened from Recents→same intent re-delivered (LAUNCHED_FROM_HISTORY)→ignored: no picker
The share waits on the router reaching /home with keyOk; it does not poll with a 12 s give-up like the notification tap (fcm:366-374). A second share replaces a waiting one. App killed before a chat is picked → the share is gone (nothing was copied, decision 7).

S3

The "Send to" picker (C18; D1–D12)

R14 bar with ✕ (Cancel sharing), title Send to, the share summary under it. R8 search Search chats. R9 header Chats, R6 rows in home order (pinned first, then most recent), no time, no unread badge; a group's subtitle is its member count. One chat per share (decision 3).
S4

Picker states

Opening 2 of 5 is the copy into app storage, which starts only after the tap (decision 7). ✕ or Back during it stops the copy and deletes what was copied. Items that fail to copy: 1 item couldn't be opened and was left out. After the copy, the chat replaces the picker.
S5

In the chat: documents confirm, text goes to the composer (E1–E5, C5)

Photos and videos then run exactly the in-app flow: one item → editor → quality sheet; two or more → album editor → one quality sheet → album. Shared text is never sent by itself: it lands in the composer (after a draft, on a new line) and you tap Send.

S8

Where shared files live (F1–F6)

share arrives: links + names + sizes only→chat tapped→copy to cache/share_in/<n>/<name> (private, ≤100 MB)→photo/video: re-encoded to a new temp (GPS stripped) · document: moved into the blob store by the send→share_in/ deleted
Also deleted when the picker is closed, at every app start (before takeShare) and at logout next to wipeMediaCache. Never external storage, never the media store. A photo shared as a file keeps its metadata, as an in-app Document pick does today (F6). Shared text lives only in the composer's in-memory draft.
