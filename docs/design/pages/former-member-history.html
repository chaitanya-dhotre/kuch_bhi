Why a server time limit is required. PocketBase compares a joined collection safely only with "any" operators; a bare <= erased real history before (76 → 0 rows). Without a limit a former member gets every later message's sender and time, live. After a leave the group probably keeps the key the leaver holds (S8), so they could read it.
