Live demos for motion.md. Press a button to play; "Reduced motion" shows what the same step does with animations turned off. Timings are the real ones. Curves here are CSS; the springs are close stand-ins for the Flutter ones.

Today a new bubble appears in one frame and everything above jumps. Send (M-5 B): the typed text leaves the field in a copy of the field's box; over 300 ms on curveMove it travels to the bubble's place while its size, corners and fill turn into the bubble's. The row opens over the same 300 ms so the list glides up, and the real bubble swaps in when the copy lands. The field clears on the tap. A second send snaps the first to its end. Long text (over 4 lines), media, voice, stickers and sends while scrolled up use the simple version: the row opens over 200 ms while the bubble rises 12 dp and fades in. Incoming bubbles rise 8 dp from the left in 180 ms. History never animates.
