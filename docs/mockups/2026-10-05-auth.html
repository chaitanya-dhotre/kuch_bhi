Batch 3 notes

Palette

Primary buttons, links, focus borders, and the PIN tile ride Marigold accent with onAccent text. Errors and snackbars use t.error. Indigo AppColors is gone from these five files.

Inputs

AuthTextField fills t.surface with a t.line border in every theme, replacing the violet-tinted surfaceContainerHighest fill. Focused border is 1.5px accent.

Mascot slot

The KB monogram on Login becomes the Capybara slot, same rig as the approved empty state. Slot not decision, the mascot art stays swappable.

Scope

Login, Register, Google onboarding, Forgot password, Backup PIN: palette and chrome only. Auth logic, providers, validators, and navigation are untouched.
